HOW IT WORKS

Simple Process. Direct Impact.

A systematic framework converting good intentions into verifiable elder support.

01

Discover a Need

Browse verified requirement lists published directly by partner care homes.

02

Pledge What is Needed

Commit to the exact quantity required, preventing duplicate shipments and overstocking.

03

Deliver or Dispatch

Use confirmed facility logistics details to deliver or ship items directly to the care home.

04

Track Receipt & Impact

Care homes verify receipt, updating live inventory meters and completion milestones.

WHY AASHRAY?

Because Dignity Is Not a Donation. It Is a Right.

Standard charitable drives often create imbalance: excess non-perishables in one location while critical medical supplies and specialized hygiene products remain unavailable elsewhere.

Aashray operates on a targeted, requirement-first model.

✓

100% Verified Demands
Every requirement is validated with care home administrators before listing.

✓

Zero-Waste Distribution
Real-time capacity tracking locks item pledges once target quantities are met.

✓

Holistic Engagement
Facilitating companionship, digital literacy, and routine health assistance alongside essential goods.
